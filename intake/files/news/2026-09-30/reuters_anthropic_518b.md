## 발췌

Anthropic said it expects to spend at least $518 billion over a decade building AI infrastructure with six partners, according to a confidential IPO prospectus seen by Reuters.

About 80% of that sum is non-cancelable or requires payment regardless of usage. Access to computing power is the key constraint; future demand is "limited principally by the availability of compute."

Plans to spend at least $111.1 billion with Alphabet's Google, $110 billion with Amazon and $31.4 billion with Microsoft under long-term infrastructure service obligations over the next seven to 10 years "regardless of usage."

Separately carrying about $161.2 billion of Broadcom-related equipment lease obligations that are largely non-cancelable.

Google: April 2026–July 2033. Amazon: May 2026–April 2036. "If our actual spend falls short, we must pay Google the difference," with similar terms on Amazon.

Microsoft $31.4 billion between November 2026 and May 2033 "is non-cancelable except in the event of Microsoft's uncured material breach."

Broadcom lease arrangements non-cancelable by either party except in the case of a default.

xAI: up to $84.5 billion of spending for Nvidia-based computing capacity through 2029, largely cancelable with 90-day notice.

AMD: committed to buying up to $5 billion of Anthropic stock and to supply AI computing capacity expected to exceed $20 billion.

Comparable in scale to OpenAI's Stargate, a $500 billion plan.

Shifting from a cloud-only model toward dedicated data centers and directly leased chips.

Amazon, Google and Microsoft are investors, customers, cloud providers, distributors and competitors; incentives "may not be fully aligned."
