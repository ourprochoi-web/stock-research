https://www.constellationenergy.com/news/2026/10/google-and-constellation-announce-landmark-agreement-to-bring-890-mw-of-new-nuclear-capacity-to-pjm-grid.html

20-year power purchase agreement for 890 MW of new nuclear capacity on the PJM grid. Investments at 11 Constellation-owned nuclear units in Illinois, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey. More than $4.3 billion of new investment by Constellation. First uprate expected by 2028. Separate 15-year energy supply agreement for an additional 2,700 MW in PJM's fleet: a financial structure so existing operating assets keep delivering energy and capacity to PJM, not a named new fuel source. Direct response to PJM's Bring Your Own Power proposal. Demand-response / load-shaping clause included. Price is not disclosed. Six sites. About 4,400 existing jobs and about 7,200 construction jobs. Also a five-year Google Cloud and Gemini Enterprise technology alliance.
