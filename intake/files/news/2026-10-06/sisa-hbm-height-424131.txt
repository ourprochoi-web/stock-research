Verified on the page:
- JEDEC max package height: HBM3E 720㎛, HBM4 775㎛.
- Supplied/target tighter than the spec: HBM3E about 715㎛, HBM4 target 770㎛ (about 5㎛ under the spec).
- NVIDIA is asking memory vendors to go lower. AMD and other customers are described as making the same request.
- Cause given: height variance damaged some HBM during final-package grinding.
- HBM4 orders skew to 8-hi more than expected. Samsung HBM4 supply is described as mostly 8-hi even though 12-hi yield is said to be stable.
- HBM4E 8-hi share may be large. Samsung and SK hynix had prioritized 16-hi and are retuning some equipment specs.
- A source says Jensen Huang asked HBM vendors directly to apply hybrid copper bonding sooner. The same source says this is not an immediate switch of all bonding.
- Samsung internally targets HCB on HBM4E, keeping TC for current volume.
- SK hynix keeps TC and is developing die-to-wafer hybrid bonding with multiple tool vendors.
