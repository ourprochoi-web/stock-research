## Claims as stated (grade hint ③, bill text unread)

- The expected mandatory tender was 100%. The version that advanced is 50%+1 plus a controlling-shareholder advance purchase.
- Of 35 control transactions since 2015, about 43% had a controlling shareholder at or above 50%, so a minority tender would often not occur in practice.
- KOSPI 200: a controlling shareholder in about 93% of names, average stake about 44%, residual shared with minorities about 6%.
- Plenary on 2026-10-01 was deferred after opposition from about 18 asset managers.
- Align Partners, as stated: accepts 50%+1, accepts pro-rata allocation, rejects the controlling-shareholder advance purchase.
