Dear Axel,

 

I am delighted to confirm that the Fluence Energy, Inc. (“Fluence”) Board of Directors (“Board”) approved you as the Interim President & Chief Executive Officer (“CEO”). This role is effective November 2, 2026. In this position you will participate in, or be eligible for, the compensation and benefits outlined below.

 

COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

 

Base Salary: Your annual base salary will be $1,015,000, pro-rated for partial years of service, effective November 2, 2026, paid in one-twelfth monthly increments, subject to applicable withholdings.

 

Discretionary Bonus: You will be eligible to receive a discretionary cash bonus for successful completion of the transition and the placement of a permanent President & CEO. Your potential receipt of this bonus is subject to approval by the Board.

 

Benefits: You will be eligible to participate in Fluence’s Health and Welfare Benefits Programs and Fluence’s short-term and long-term disability, accidental death and dismemberment, and life insurance plans, in accordance with their terms (as may be amended from time to time).

 

Retirement: You will be eligible to participate in the Fluence Energy Savings Plan (the “401(k) plan”). You may contribute up to 50% of your salary to your account and will be eligible to receive a company matching contribution of 100% of your contributions up to 5% of your gross salary, in each case, in accordance with the terms and conditions of the plan.

 

Severance: Given the interim nature of your position, you will not be designated as a participant in the Fluence Executive Severance Plan and, as such, you will not be eligible to receive severance thereunder.
