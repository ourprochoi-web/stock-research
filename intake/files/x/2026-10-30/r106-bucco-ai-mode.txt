Reddit call was absolutely fascinating. Everyone should listen to it as a bellwether for where the internet is going
- AI Mode is increasingly default Google experience
- AI Mode provides rich info and data in line, less incentive to leave search
- AI Mose incentivizes dialog, Google wants to keep you engaged to serve more ads at each turn 
- Google will milk search for as much revenue as possible as they transition from a search company to a cloud service and chip company
- As traffic from humans becomes more scarce this will put Google in an increasingly adversarial position to the rest of the internet
- This is a massive headwind to all media type companies who are not destinations
- This is a massive headwind to all content-driven go-to-market motions especially in the SMB. You have seen companies like HubSpot and Monday.com get crushed by this funnel collapse 

We are entering the end game - a knife fight for the remaining and dwindling volume of human traffic on the internet

What this is not likely, but we do not have visibility into this, is the sort of algorithm change where I would say, "Hey, these things come and go." This appears to be really the rollout of AI Mode, and maybe a change in the machine translation or how they treat that. Reddit, however, is still a top search term in Google, so this does not say anything about the internet consumers' demand for Reddit. People still want Reddit. It is just Google is not sending them to Reddit the way they used to. Given our strategy is to make Reddit a direct destination, this is actually in line with our strategy because Google is teaching people that if you want to come to Reddit, you need to come to Reddit.
