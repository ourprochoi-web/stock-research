• Indium Phosphide (InP) Substrates: (🔥 Record Revenue Driver)
  - AI datacenter optical interconnects (800G, 1.6T, and Co-Packaged Optics / CPO) driving surging demand across tier-1 optical module customers.
• Gallium Arsenide (GaAs) & Germanium (Ge):
  - Satellite solar cell applications and wireless infrastructure demand remained steady.
• Export Permits & Shipments:
  - China facility export permit processing tracked smoothly during the quarter, allowing permit-backed backlogs to convert into recognized deliveries.

💡 KEY TAKEAWAYS & EARNINGS CALL HIGHLIGHTS

1. AI Optical Supercycle Confirmed:
   - Hyperscalers expanding AI clusters require massive bandwidth, creating an acute demand inflection for high-speed InP optical components that continues to outpace supply.
2. Rapid Operating Leverage & Margin Expansion:
   - Non-GAAP gross margin expanded to 56.1% (up from 45.0% in Q2), demonstrating strong fixed-cost absorption and favorable product mix toward high-spec InP wafers.
3. Capacity Expansion Progress:
   - Management noted that previously funded InP capacity additions are ramping up efficiently to meet backlogs extending into 2027.
