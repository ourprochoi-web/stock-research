Broadcom's substrate joint venture with TOPPAN has opened its new plant in Singapore. Advanced Substrate Technologies (AST) is the first facility in Singapore able to produce high-end FC-BGA substrates, and the TOPPAN Group's first such site outside Japan. Broadcom is TOPPAN's largest FC-BGA substrate customer. 1. About 95,000 square metres in the Jurong Lake District 2. Ground broken in March 2024, brought online three months early 3. More than 200 engineers and technicians hired so far 4. High-value manufacturing targeted for late 2026 The plant will make large-body, high-layer-count substrates for AI compute and networking chips. The release calls advanced substrate capacity a persistent bottleneck for those chips, with demand still outpacing global supply. Singapore Deputy Prime Minister Gan Kim Yong attended the opening ceremony. Charlie Kawwas, President of Broadcom's Semiconductor Solutions Group: "This facility, brought online three months early, strengthens our supply chain resilience by delivering vital relief while the industry is constrained by substrate shortages."
