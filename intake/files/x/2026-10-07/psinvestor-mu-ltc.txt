Micron has more customers asking for long term contracts than it is willing to sign.
JPMorgan investor call with Satya Kumar, Micron head of investor relations and treasury.
The contract book already covers more than a third of revenue through 2030, customers are extending it into 2031, and MU still plans to stop around half. The rest sells on annual or as-needed terms.
The extra bits Micron pulled out of existing fabs in 2025 and 2026 are used up, and new supply now needs new cleanrooms. "The towel is fully squeezed," Kumar said, per the note.
About $1B of the fiscal Q1 cost step-up stays for the whole year, so every point of margin gain in fiscal 2027 has to come from price and mix. HBM contracts reset higher on January 1.
Micron has about $2B left on its buyback authorization. JPMorgan models more than $100B of free cash flow in the three quarters after the CHIPS restriction lapses on December 9. Kumar said some restrictions stay after that date. The larger authorization Micron says is coming has not been announced yet.
