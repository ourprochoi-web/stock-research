$TEM says its AI can read a routine lung cancer slide and flag which patients need biomarker testing, catching more than 80% of positive cases for all 12 biomarkers it checked.

The study covered 60,459 advanced NSCLC cases from 56,969 patients and came out of Tempus' collaboration with $PFE Pfizer.

Sensitivity tells you how many true cases it catches. The release doesn't give specificity, how many patients get flagged who don't carry the biomarker. That's the number that decides whether this saves tests or adds them.

Tempus already sells Paige Predict, which reads the same H&E slide for 123 biomarkers across 16 cancer types. This NSCLC panel is still in development and isn't meant to replace molecular testing.

It's a poster at ESMO in Madrid next week.
