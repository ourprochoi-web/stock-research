$KOPN and $FABC put out a second paper on Neural I/o today.

GPUs talk to each other over copper, and copper only holds up for about a meter. That's why the tightly linked group of GPUs ends at one rack.

Their idea is to send that data as light from tiny MicroLEDs, the same kind of part $KOPN builds for displays, across many channels at once. Light carries farther, so the linked group could stretch across a whole row.

The gain they model comes from GPUs spending less time waiting on each other. The chips themselves don't get faster.

All of it is modeled. Link testing has started, the public demo is targeted for January and first production silicon for 2028.

$KOPN owns 19.9% of $FABC and would make the chips, so this is also the two companies that stand to sell it making the case.
