## Facts to preserve

- SASAC surveyed Broadcom (AVGO) switch use in state-owned/state-controlled data centers in recent weeks. **Confirmed per FT reporting**; there is no official circular, and Reuters said it could not immediately verify independently.
- Possible informal guidance to scale back Broadcom is **partial**: no formal ban or confirmed policy shift was reported.
- In some SOEs, AVGO switches were described as **“as much as 90%” of installed switches**. This is **partial**, based on an anonymous preliminary FT account.
- FT reported that NVIDIA (NVDA) was already excluded from SOE data centers. “AVGO is the last non-China supplier” is an interpretation, not a complete supplier fact: Huawei, H3C, and Ruijie also exist. Private companies (including ByteDance and Alibaba) were outside the potential constraint scope in the relay.
- The AVGO market-cap/earnings story remains primarily the **ASIC/XPU** axis. Reporting and filings do not provide a switch/SOE revenue breakout. FY2025 China + Hong Kong deliveries of approximately **$11.2B / ~17%** are a regional aggregate, not a switch or SOE split; preserve this as disclosure framing only.
- Broadcom and SASAC had no immediate comment.
