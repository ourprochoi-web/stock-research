$SLNH and $APLD are forming a JV for a 50MW AI data center at Dorothy 3 in Texas, next to the 150MW Briscoe wind farm Soluna bought in April.

Soluna puts in the land and power and keeps 49%. Applied puts in the capital and takes 51%. The planned tenant is $CHRN, which Applied majority owns.

This is how real estate gets built when the landowner is small. You contribute the dirt, the partner brings the money, and you skip the dilution.
