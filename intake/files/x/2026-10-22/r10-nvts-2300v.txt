Solar farms and grid batteries run on 1500 V DC buses. 1200 V switches can't hold that alone, so designers stack them in three-level converters. A 2300 V switch lets the same converter run two-level, with fewer devices and fewer gate drivers. $WOLF pitched its own 2300 V modules on that same math in 2024.

Per the datasheet, the 8 mΩ is die-only at 25°C. At 175°C it rises to 21 mΩ, and the package adds about 1.9 mΩ per half-bridge. Converters get sized to the hot figure.

1. Q2 revenue was $10.5M, Q3 guide is $13.5M
2. Management expects AI data center plus grid and energy to pass one-third of revenue by year-end
3. $NVTS sizes wide-bandgap content in solid-state transformers at $25K to $35K per MW

At $25K per MW, 1 GW of SST-fed data center capacity is $25M of content for all suppliers combined. That is about 2.4x $NVTS's Q2 revenue (my math).

Samples are the start of a design-in. Grid equipment qualifies slowly, and $NVTS has put SSTs inside AI data centers at 2028 and later.
