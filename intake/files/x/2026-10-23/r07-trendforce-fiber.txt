## #567 semiconsight · 2026-10-22 23:30 KST · original · https://x.com/semiconsight/status/2113276871425368409
$GLW $VZ  
As AI data center spending grows, the first part to run short is, surprisingly, one of the oldest. Optical fiber, drawn from melted glass into a thread. Fiber used for cabling inside data centers is expected to cost more than five times its 2025 price in 2026.

Taiwanese research firm TrendForce expects the world to be short 180 million fiber-km of fiber in 2026. A fiber-km is the length of a single fiber strand measured in kilometers. Plants are already running almost nonstop, and only a month of inventory is left.

The biggest price increase is in a fiber called BIF. It loses little signal even when bent around tight spaces, and it's used for cabling inside data centers. That reads as densely packed AI data centers leading the price rise.

The problem is that supply can't ramp quickly. Fiber is made by melting a thick glass rod, called a preform, and drawing it out. Expanding preform output requires new equipment and a rebuilt process, which takes 18–24 months.

Capacity approved today won't reach the market until around 2028. It's the same math behind TrendForce's view that the shortage lasts until at least 2028.

So large buyers are locking up volume first. In September 2026, $VZ signed a multi-year agreement with $GLW for fiber deliveries from 2027 through 2032. The more volume gets tied up in long-term contracts, the less is left for everyone else.
