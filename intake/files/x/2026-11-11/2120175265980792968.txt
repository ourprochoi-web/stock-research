Richard Choe's revenue and EBITDA estimates moved less than 3%. The target still fell 15%.

His DCF runs at a 9% discount rate. At $20.94 this morning, $IREN trades closer to his 10% row, so the market is charging more for money than his model does (my math).

His model bills less per MW than the deals $IREN is signing now. My guess is that's partly the ramp and partly caution. Newer GPUs also cost more per MW, so not all of a higher price reaches margin.

Choe says prepayments usually come from weaker credits paying up front to lock in capacity. Hyperscalers are now open to about 90% prepaid on $IREN's Vera Rubin capacity, due in the second half of 2027. None has signed on those terms yet.

Each deal like the new 5-year one, with 90% of the GPU cost prepaid, means less borrowing for the GPUs.
