$QS is taking its solid-state lithium-metal cells into AI data centers.

QuantumScape introduced the $QS PowerBlock this morning, a modular in-rack battery system designed for 800VDC architecture and industry-standard power rack configurations.

Against current OCP data center specifications, the company cites:
1. 4x the power density
2. 5x the runtime
3. Cell thermal stability well over 300°C in third-party and customer safety testing, vs about 180°C where conventional lithium-ion typically hits thermal runaway

The density and runtime figures come from prototype testing and modeled configurations. Final performance depends on product design and real-world conditions.
