$CAT Wells Fargo's 4Q26 machinery outlook has Caterpillar's turbine lead times stretching about a year further during Q3, out into 2030.

Recip engine lead times added a net 3 months over the quarter. Wells Fargo also discloses they shortened by about 3 months from August to September.

Reported backlog runs shorter than the real queue. Caterpillar reserves production slots for long-term customers under framework agreements, and those slots only become orders close to the ship date.

My guess is backlog keeps building for several quarters even if new framework deals slowed, because the reserved slots keep converting on schedule. Wells Fargo models another quarter of 1.3x+ book-to-bill.
