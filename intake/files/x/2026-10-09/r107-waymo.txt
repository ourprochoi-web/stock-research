Waymo, the $GOOGL robotaxi unit, has closed its $5 billion term loan. It is Waymo's first debt financing.

Oct 6 the deal got upsized from just over $3 billion, priced at 5.25 percentage points over the benchmark. Waymo confirmed the close today.

1. Lead syndicated lenders: PIMCO, Blackstone and Sixth Street
2. Significant lenders: Capital Group, Loomis Sayles and T. Rowe Price
3. Sole lead bookrunner: Goldman Sachs

TechCrunch also lists Apollo, Blue Owl, Fidelity, Franklin Templeton, HPS and Oaktree among the lenders.

Waymo called the financing a step in its evolution into a "𝙨𝙘𝙖𝙡𝙞𝙣𝙜 𝙘𝙤𝙢𝙢𝙚𝙧𝙘𝙞𝙖𝙡 𝙚𝙣𝙩𝙚𝙧𝙥𝙧𝙞𝙨𝙚."

It raised $16 billion in equity in February at a $126 billion valuation, and Alphabet remains the majority investor. Waymo runs robotaxi service in 15 markets and is testing in London and Tokyo.
