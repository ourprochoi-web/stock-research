- "As of September 3, 2026, the transaction price allocated to our remaining performance obligations was approximately $134 billion, of which $13 billion has been recognized as contract liabilities. Contract liabilities primarily consisted of customer deposits associated with strategic customer agreements. Nearly all of the deposits are scheduled to be repaid between 2029 and 2031. Approximately one-fourth of the remaining performance obligations as of September 3, 2026 are expected to be recognized as revenue over the next twelve months. The remaining performance obligations are expected to be fully recognized within the next five years. As of August 28, 2025, our remaining performance obligations were not material."

## 방송 숫자와 대조 (이형수 10/18 삼프로)
- RPO 「1,500억 달러」 → 10-K ① 약 1,340억 달러(가격 고정·밴드 없는 계약 제외). 1,500억은 콜 발언(r-20261001-01 ②)과 같은 자릿수.
- 선수금 「220억 달러」 → 10-K ①: FY26 현금 예치금 수취 127.5억 + 신용장 70억 = 197.5억 달러. 계약부채 130억. 「220억」과 정확히 맞는 항목 없음. 콜의 「약정 320억」(r-20261001-01 ②)과도 다름.
- 「계약가격 하단이 과거 슈퍼사이클 상단보다 높다」 → 10-K는 하한·상한 밴드 존재만 적고 수준은 없음. 미검증.
