## Reasons — capital raising

> The Special Committee believes that the Recapitalization, the adoption of the Restated Charter, and the declaration and payment of the Dividend, and entering into the Founder Alignment Agreement with Mr. Zuckerberg in accordance with the terms thereof, are advisable, fair to and in the best interests of the Company and our shareholders in order to (i) create a publicly traded, non-voting equity security that the Company may use for capital raising, employee compensation, potential acquisitions, and other general corporate purposes, in each case, without diluting the voting power of the existing Class A and Class B common stock; (ii) establish a deep and liquid trading market for Class C common stock designed to minimize any trading-value discount relative to the Class A common stock; (iii) in addition to the initial number of shares of Class C common stock that are freely transferable immediately following the Dividend, ensure that Mr. Zuckerberg’s ability to freely transfer his economic interest in his remaining shares of Class C common stock from the Dividend is permitted only in compliance with the Stapling Condition, with a number of shares equal to 2.5% of the shares of Class C common stock issued to Mr. Zuckerberg in the Dividend released from such conditions for each full calendar quarter during which the Continued Leadership Condition is satisfied at all times; (iv) ensure that future dividends of Class C common stock pursuant to the Founder Alignment Agreement are subject to the Company’s achievement of extraordinary stock price targets, and, with respect to Mr. Zuckerberg, subject to the Stapling Condition for a number of shares of Class C common stock equal to the Post-Leadership Stapling Number, unless the Continued Leadership Condition is satisfied as of the date of the event giving rise to such dividend; and (v) continue to align with the determinations historically made by the Board, acting through its Compensation, Nominating & Governance 

> The Special Committee determined that the ability to issue non-voting shares of Class C common stock via the Recapitalization and the Dividend will provide us with the critical ability to raise equity capital to finance our evolution and growth, and help us remain a leader among fierce competition domestically and from foreign competitors. The scale of investment required to compete, sustain our growth, expand our infrastructure, and pursue new strategic initiatives amidst the dramatic transformation taking place in the tech industry and global economy related to artificial intelligence is unprecedented. The ability to raise equity capital through issuances of Class C common stock will allow us access to the equity markets to meet our capital requirements without diluting the voting power of our current shareholders or affecting Mr. Zuckerberg’s leadership or his current control of the Company (discussed more fully below), which is in the best interests of all shareholders in light of his track record of creating exceptional value for Company shareholders. The Special Committee considered whether the Company could issue Class A common stock in order to raise capital, but Mr. Zuckerberg made it clear during the course of the negotiations with the Special Committee that he would not support a significant equity financing that would have the effect of reducing his voting power in the Company. The Special Committee also considered whether debt financing could be an alternative for the financing needs of the Company but recognized that debt financing alternatives of the magnitude that Company management was considering could have an adverse effect on the Company’s credit ratings and would be a less attractive alternative for the Company.

> The Special Committee also recognized that timing is important to secure capital before competitors saturate various funding options. Recent significant capital raises at other notable companies, and others soon anticipated to take place, to fund the same AI growth—for example, Alphabet, SpaceX, Anthropic, and OpenAI—have resulted in an increasingly challenging environment for raising capital. The Special Committee considered that, while the Company presently has sufficient capital resources for its current needs, in order to finance its funding needs going forward it would be prudent for the Company to do so before even more companies have absorbed available capital in the market. In addition, management had conveyed to the Special Committee a desire for the Class C common stock to be available for use as early as the end of 2026. The Special Committee determined that it is preferable to implement the Recapitalization and Dividend now, so that we are positioned to act with speed and flexibility when capital needs or strategic opportunities arise and so that, with the Dividend, there will be a liquid market for the Class C common stock. The Company expects to use shares of Class C common stock to conduct equity offerings from time to time in the future, subject to market conditions.
