Item 1.01. On October 5, 2026 Netlist entered a Settlement and Release Agreement with Micron Technology, Inc. which settles and mutually releases all pending legal proceedings between the Company and Micron identified therein.

Patent License Agreement, same date: Netlist grants Micron a worldwide, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable five-year term license to Netlist's entire worldwide patent portfolio.

Payments: quarterly license fees of $30 million for each of the twenty calendar quarters from the fourth calendar quarter of 2026 through the third calendar quarter of 2031, total value $600 million.

Securities Purchase Agreement and Lock-Up and Release Agreement, same date: Micron purchased 10 million shares of Netlist common stock for an aggregate cash purchase price of $1 million. Twenty percent of the shares are released from transfer restrictions on each of the first, second, third and fourth anniversaries, remainder on the fifth anniversary. Unregistered, Section 4(a)(2). Closing on or before October 6, 2026.
