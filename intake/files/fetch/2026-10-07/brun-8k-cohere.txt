Item 1.01. On September 30, 2026, Boost Run LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Boost Run Inc., entered into a Service Agreement with Cohere Inc. Boost Run will provide access to dedicated GPU cloud computing infrastructure and related services.
The term for each rack is approximately five years, commencing upon Cohere's acceptance of that rack. Acceptance of the initial infrastructure is currently expected to begin in the second quarter of 2027.
Subject to delivery and acceptance requirements and any termination described, Cohere has committed to pay approximately $525.6 million over the term, a portion as a prepayment.
If a specified minimum amount of infrastructure has not been accepted by July 15, 2027, Cohere may terminate and receive a refund of all amounts prepaid. If the minimum has been accepted, Cohere may terminate the order for infrastructure not accepted by that date and receive a refund of the related prepaid amounts. The parent guarantees Boost Run's refund obligation up to the prepayment received and not applied against fees.

The 8-K does not name NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 and does not state a $2.6 billion cumulative contract value.
