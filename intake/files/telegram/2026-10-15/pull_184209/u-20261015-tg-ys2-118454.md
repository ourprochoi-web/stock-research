## N2 · margin · capex execution

Gross margin decreased by 1.3 percentage points sequentially to 66.4%, primarily due to margin dilution from 2-nanometer ramp.
2-nanometer process technology contributed 8% of wafer revenue in the third quarter, while 3-nanometer, 5-nanometer, and 7-nanometer accounted for 33%, 28%, and 10% respectively.
HPC increased 22% quarter over quarter to account for 68% of our third quarter revenue.
In US dollar terms, our third quarter capital expenditures totaled US$13.8 billion.
we expect our fourth quarter revenue to be between US$52.3 and US$54.3 billion ... based on the exchange rate assumption of US$1 to NT$31.8.
our third quarter gross margin decreased by 130 basis points sequentially to 66.4%, primarily due to the margin dilution from the initial ramp-up of our 2-nanometer technology.
fourth quarter gross margin to decrease by 40 basis points to 66% at the midpoint, primarily due to the continued margin dilution from the steep ramp-up of our 2-nanometer technology as well as dilution from overseas fabs.
we now expect the gross margin dilution from the ramp-up of our overseas fabs to be closer to 2% for the full year 2026.
we expect the dilution from our overseas fabs to widen to 3% to 4% in the next few years.
we reiterate our full-year 2026 capital budget to be between US$60 and US$64 billion.
we now expect our full-year 2026 revenue growth to be around 44% year over year in US dollar terms.
we continue to observe consumer and price-sensitive end market segments being challenged due to the impact of rising prices and macroeconomic uncertainties.
N2 has already entered high-volume manufacturing in the first half of 2025. With good yield, N2 is ramping successfully in multiple phases at both Hsinchu and Kaohsiung sites. We expect our N2 Family Technologies annual capacity to more than double by 2027, supported by strong demand from both smartphone and HPC AI applications.
A14 ... Risk production is well on track for 2027 and volume production will start in 2028.
add three new fabs to support ... 3-nanometer ... Volume production for our new 3-nanometer fab in Tainan Science Park is well on track to begin in the first half of 2027.
