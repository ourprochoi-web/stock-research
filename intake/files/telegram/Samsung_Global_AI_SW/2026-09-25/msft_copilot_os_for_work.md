## Claim summary

Microsoft’s new Copilot is described as three surfaces: Home combines Chat and Cowork with Word/Excel/PowerPoint embedded; Code turns natural-language requests into apps, dashboards, and workflows with GitHub Copilot and Managed Runtime; and Autopilot (formerly Scout) provides identity, memory, computer use, and an always-on agent. Microsoft IQ, Power BI’s 20M+ semantic models, Dynamics 365/Power Platform, and a Plugin Registry provide the context and extension layer. The monetization split is user-subscription/USL for Chat and Office versus usage-based billing (UBB) plus FinOps for Cowork, Code, Autopilot, and frontier products such as Astra/Fable. A proactive Today experience for email, calendar, and Teams is expected in October.
