China blocks SoftBank's US$5.4 billion takeover of ABB's robotics division, FT reports
China has told SoftBank it will not allow the US$5.4 billion (HK$42.12 billion) acquisition of ABB’s robotics division to go ahead, the Financial Times reported on Thursday, citing multiple people familiar with the matter.
Reuters could not immediately verify the report.
