# K7 — Novo STEP UP liver-fat subgroup at EASD 2026 (collection, not a judgment)

Grade hint ②. Channel note plus a reprint of a company release. Not a filing. Not an approval.

- Routed source: https://t.me/insidertracking/65131
- Channel paraphrase: post-hoc STEP UP subgroup. Injectable Wegovy (semaglutide 7.2 mg and 2.4 mg) reduced liver fat over 72 weeks in adults with obesity and without diabetes. Mean liver fat 8.8% to 3.1%. Among participants starting above 5%, 88.5% were below 5% at week 72. n=55; 26 started above 5%; 23 of those reached below 5%. Presented at EASD 2026 in Milan. Eric Lawitz (Texas Liver Institute and UT Health San Antonio) is quoted on addressing liver disease early.

- Reprint figures that match the channel: MRI liver fat in the pooled semaglutide 7.2 mg and 2.4 mg group from 8.8% at baseline to 3.1% at week 72; 26 participants entered above 5% and 23 (88.5%) reached below 5%. The release calls the analysis post hoc and exploratory, and says liver measures were not a pre-specified primary or secondary endpoint of the parent trials. It also says semaglutide 2.4 mg is approved for MASH in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, China, and other markets, and that semaglutide 7.2 mg is not being investigated for MASH.
