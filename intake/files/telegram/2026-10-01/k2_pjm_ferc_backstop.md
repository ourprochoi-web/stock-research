# K2 — FERC defers PJM data-center reliability backstop (collection, not a judgment)

- Telegram: https://t.me/YeouidoStory2/118022 (2026-09-30 23:18 widget). Headline links https://n.news.naver.com/mnews/article/029/0003050868
- Article: Digital Times, filed 2026-10-01 07:23 KST. Grade hint ②. FERC order text was not opened.

FERC accepted PJM's Reliability Backstop Procurement filing and deferred implementation until 28 Feb 2027, while taking written comment on cost allocation and long-term contract risk. A revised PJM filing could shorten the deferral.

Trigger: the July auction for delivery year 2028–29 cleared 149.2 GW and missed the reliability requirement by 6,831 MW (about 6.8 GW). PJM had proposed a one-time procurement with contracts up to 15 years. The dispute is whether the cost and risk of that long-term power, bought for data centers, would be shifted onto other customers. The auction cleared at the cap of $325/MW-day; procurement cost about $16.4 billion.
