Source: MacRumors / Joe Rossignol / Friday October 16, 2026 6:11 am PDT
URL: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/16/iphone-duo-sold-out-for-christmas/

Apple's U.S. online store is already showing an estimated shipping date of January 25 for all iPhone Duo configurations following the start of pre-orders today (2026-10-16). Christmas gift inventory is effectively sold out for online orders.

Some Apple Stores may have units for walk-in customers on Friday, October 23 launch day, but inventory is expected to be very tight. Apple Store pickup reservation: online store says to check back on Saturday, October 24.

Production: iPhone Duo reportedly facing production difficulties ahead of October launch; early output expected to be limited.
