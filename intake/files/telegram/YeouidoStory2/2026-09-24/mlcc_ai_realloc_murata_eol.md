## Claim summary

The relay says Japanese and Korean MLCC makers are reducing consumer supply to prioritize AI. Chinese suppliers are entering global-brand qualification through Dell, HP, ASUS, and Acer ODM routes involving Huaqin, LCFC, and Luxshare; Quanta and Pegatron were described as visiting Chaozhou Three-Circle.

Murata reportedly said on September 10 that it plans to discontinue some consumer general-purpose GRM products and some automotive products, typically categories with **GPM below 5%**, while focusing on high-spec small-size, high-capacitance MLCC for AI servers/data centers and automotive applications. Orders for the affected products are accepted through 2028 and final shipments end in 2029, implying roughly a three-year transition intended to avoid a 2018-style shortage.

The mid-term read-through is tighter mid/high-cap consumer MLCC as AI capacity receives priority, with some demand shifting to China. China is unlikely to threaten Japanese/Korean suppliers in high-spec AI-server MLCC in the short term; the more likely competitive pressure is in general server, consumer, and low-spec automotive products.
