## AI hardware / semi sentiment

London investor conversations were constructive on AI hardware and semis, but showed little actual adding or re-levering into growth. Reasons cited were frontier-model misalignment and C28 funding fears, macro / politics / war risk, and investors protecting gains. The tone is interest without broad risk re-expansion.

Investors generally preferred memory, but were reluctant to re-add growth—especially with MU back above $1,000. WFE and HDD were hard to own until DRAM sentiment clearly improves. The Micron gate is reinforced: even a good MU print may sell off as a sell-the-news event, consistent with Lead's **beat largely priced_in** framing.

## Agents, CPU / GPU, and memory

Some investors are starting to use agents; a few are skipping junior hires. CPU is favored through the “agents need computers” framing, with Muse described as a free PC. AMD is preferred over INTC. There are some ARM shorts but no consensus; QCOM is mixed. The top open questions are what restarts NVDA and AVGO. NVDA sentiment is better, while U.S. investors worry more about CSP capex for AVGO than about MediaTek.

Long ideas mentioned include **MRVL and QCOM**. EDA—especially **CDNS**—was favored in the context of SaaS outperformance. Analog was mentioned in Europe without conviction. These are recorded as investor positioning only, not adds.

## Networking and optical

There is **no clear networking consensus** and little interest in laggards **CRDO or CLS**. Investors were surprised by ANET's strength. **LITE / COHR** drew more interest as NPO picks-and-shovels around ECOC and roadshows, but few investors were aggressive longs. Nokia has strong following, though there was pushback versus the U.S. preference for CIEN and its multiple premium; many U.S. hedge funds are perceived to be long Nokia.

The FCC transceiver-ban point is a **soft optical risk watch**, not a position change. London's neglect of CRDO versus LITE / COHR interest does **not** flip the residual: **SMTC > CRDO (conditional) > MXL** remains unchanged. Do not chase LITE / COHR on ECOC densification alone.
