## Claim summary

Meta tested a Muse human concierge flow in which external contractors placed some phone calls when AI calling failed. That created privacy risk because user requests and personally identifiable information could reach humans. One employee ISP/cable-negotiation call reportedly included racist remarks from a contractor. Meta’s Superintelligence Lab acknowledged insufficient disclosure, halted the test, apologized, and barred that contractor. Reuters reporting was relayed by IT Chosun.

The broader read-through is that fully autonomous agent calling is not yet reliable; human-in-the-loop execution raises disclosure, privacy, and liability requirements.
