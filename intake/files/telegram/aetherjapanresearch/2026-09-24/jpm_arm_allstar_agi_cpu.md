## Key points

- AI/DC demand remains strong across license/royalty and merchant AGI CPU. Cloud/AI royalty was 14% of total royalty in the last fiscal year and grew about 2x YoY; Arm expects server CPU royalty to exceed 10% of total royalty in FY27.
- The opportunity also includes SmartNIC/DPU, networking chips with Arm cores, and eSSD controllers.
- Arm's merchant 3nm AGI CPU remains on track for an initial ramp above $100M in F4Q27 (the March 2027 quarter).
- After the last earnings report, Arm secured additional supply, increasing confidence in more than $1B of AGI CPU chip revenue across FY27–FY28 on firm orders. Demand outlook remains above $2B. Meta is a major customer.
- Gross margin may run below the high-30s target if wafer, substrate, and packaging costs are higher; management is prioritizing share over gross margin.
- The 2nm AGI design remains on track for early H2 CY27.
- Ethos NPU adoption in MCU/MPU products could provide a 15–20% embedded royalty tailwind.
