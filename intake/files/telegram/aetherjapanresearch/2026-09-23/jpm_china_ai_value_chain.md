## Facts to preserve

### Market size and AI investment

- China inference token consumption is modeled at roughly **60×** 2026 levels by 2030, with about **69% enterprise** and **31% consumer** consumption in 2030.
- Global AI end-customer spend is estimated at **more than $1.4T in 2030**, with about one-quarter in China. China demand is modeled as roughly **$250B B2B** (**agents ~$147B** plus **tools / copilot / AI SaaS ~$96B**) and **$100B consumer**.
- China hyperscaler AI investment rises from **$100B in 2026** by **40% in 2027**, **20% in 2028**, and **10% in 2029**, then plateaus at **$184.8B in 2030**; five-year cumulative investment is **$777.6B**.

### Triple monetization and operating-profit migration

- JPMorgan frames **$1 invested** as monetized three times: hardware at equipment purchase, cloud over the next five years, and model + applications by selling intelligence.
- 2026→2030 revenue multiples are **hardware 1.8×**, **cloud 12.5×**, and **model + applications / downstream 26.9×** (**$15.7B→$423.4B**). Industry operating profit rises from **$27B to $243B**.
- Five-year cumulative operating profit is **$544B**: approximately **53% hardware**, **16% cloud**, and **32% downstream**. However, about **64% of annual profit growth** comes from downstream.
- Hardware's profit share falls from **93% in 2026 to 30% in 2030**; downstream moves from losses to **54%** of profit. Downstream remains loss-making through 2027 and turns profitable in 2028 at **$6.2B**.
- Key assumptions are gross margin improving **30%→50%** and training falling from **45% to 15% of cloud revenue**. About **77% of downstream five-year cumulative profit is concentrated in 2030**, so funding runway is a stock-selection key and last-year figures are sensitive to assumptions.

### What the market prices and what to check

- The market assigns about **14% of China AI listed market cap** to model + applications, close to the **~12% 2026 revenue mix**. This is reasonable against the current mix, but does not yet price a shift to **49% of 2030 revenue / 54% of 2030 profit**.
- 12–24 month checks are **cloud utilization**, **recurring API / subscription revenue**, **paid users**, and **gross margin after inference cost**.
- A **$100B cloud cohort** is modeled at about **3.64 years pretax payback** and **11.6% pretax IRR**. Cloud profitability is the gate for whether value-chain investment continues.

### Opportunity / Scarcity / Value Capture

- Scarcity is expected to migrate from compute and frontier intelligence toward deployment bottlenecks: **proprietary data, context, permissions, workflows, and execution**.
- Example: the **Salesforce×Anthropic Claudeforce** collaboration (August 2026). Claude skills can run Salesforce work, but data, permissions, and record updates remain with Salesforce. Seats may fall while mechanical agent usage rises, shifting monetization toward **usage / transaction / outcome** pricing.
- Falling intelligence cost helps applications twice—by expanding automatable workflows and lowering cost per workflow—but revenue is not early profit. Seat decline may precede usage monetization.
