Reuters (Timothy Gardner; page dated October 4, 2026; anonymous person familiar): The US will lend Vistra about $4.2 billion to boost nuclear output. Energy Secretary Chris Wright will announce it on Monday at Vistra's nuclear plant on Lake Erie in Ohio. The loan would uprate at least three of Vistra's four nuclear stations, without new NRC licenses. Vistra has six reactors at four US plants generating more than 6.5 GW. DOE and Vistra did not immediately comment. Bloomberg reported it first.

Bloomberg (Ari Natter, Will Wade, Oct 02, 2026; people familiar, not authorized to speak on record): A roughly $4 billion loan package to upgrade three nuclear plants. Wright expected to announce as soon as Monday at Perry, northeast of Cleveland. Two plants in Ohio and one in Pennsylvania. Perry about 1.3 GW (one reactor), Davis-Besse about 900 MW (one unit), Beaver Valley about 1.9 GW (two units). Expansion MW was not known. Shares fell as much as 3.6% then traded 0.9% lower at $138.56 by 3:48 p.m.

DOE award press release: not found on 2026-10-05. energy.gov/articles (front page) had no Vistra loan item. energy.gov search for "Vistra loan" (content type article) did not return a Vistra loan announcement. A related DOE document, not an award, is the 2026-09-15 Office of Energy Dominance Financing Section 106 letter on a proposed loan guarantee for the Perry uprate only (https://www.energy.gov/documents/vistra-corp-perry-nuclear-power-plant-pnpp-uprate-project-nhpa-section-106-finding-no). It says construction is anticipated to begin in October 2026. It does not state the $4.2 billion figure, added MW, interest rate, or completion date. Grade stays ②.
