## Facts to preserve

- Current production mostly orders from late-2022 to 2023.
- Capacity is booked through 2028; new orders are for 2029–2030. More than half of 2029–30 is already filled, and booking continues toward 100%.
- 2023 long-term supply contracts had open quantity (room); the actual PO sets the contract details (partial quote in source).
- PPI is based on **delivered** unit prices to customers that month, **not** new-order booking prices.
- Short-lead distribution transformers have less lag; for long-lead EHV transformers, PPI and booking ASP can diverge a lot.
- Source conclusion: PPI is better for estimating quarterly realized ASP than booking ASP; booking ASP needs company/voltage-mix tracking.
