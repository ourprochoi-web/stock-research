## 4. What Kenneth should NOT chase (hype traps)

1. **“Anthropic will acquire / merge with [public biotech]” tickers** — On-record stance: no clinical trials for now; partner/tooling model; wet lab ≠ commercial pipeline. Do not front-run imaginary M&A.
2. **BenevolentAI listing nostalgia / delisted AI-KG names** — Private again; AZ target wins are real historically but not a liquid thesis.
3. **Any microcap that only has an “AI” press release + no named pharma, no data asset, no physics/ML methods paper trail** — marketing ≠ synergy.
4. **KR midcap AI-drug names (Syntekabio, Standigm, etc.) as buy candidates** — Fine as **study/watch notes**; formal KR midcap buys need Kenneth’s allow. Lunit is stronger as **imaging/medtech AI** than as drug-discovery synergy.
5. **Double-counting mega AI deals** — NVO’s OpenAI + Anthropic + NVIDIA stack is real but **does not** make NVO an “AI stock”; same for BMY. Size positions on drug fundamentals first.
6. **NVIDIA BioNeMo as a biotech pick** — Correct as **picks-and-shovels** (NVDA); wrong as a substitute for owning TEM/SDGR/RXRX biology risk.
7. **Exscientia (EXAI)** — Delisted into RXRX (Nov 2024); don’t chase legacy ticker.
