CEO (Sanjay Mehrotra), as transcribed:
- 26 SCAs signed, estimated over 35% of revenue through 2030. Three quarters of that estimated revenue has a defined pricing framework, a majority with floor and ceiling. The remaining quarter negotiated periodically on market prices.
- Financial commitments from customers on the 26 signed SCAs and extensions increased to $32 billion, the vast majority cash deposits.
- Server unit growth in the high teens percentage range in both calendar 2026 and 2027. This is server units, not DRAM ASP.
- Calendar 2027 HBM: agreements for the vast majority of calendar 2027 HBM bit supply, with significant price increases year-over-year. Q&A: a large part of 2027 HBM volume already sold, prices much higher than 2026. Label is calendar 2027, not fiscal.
- Supply-demand much tighter in calendar 2027 and 2028 than in 2026. Industry expected to remain supply constrained in both years for DRAM and NAND. No line of sight to balance.
- FY27 CapEx to increase vs prior plans; a majority of the increase is construction CapEx.

CFO (Mark Murphy), as transcribed:
- RPO approximately $150 billion. Take-or-pay volumes. RPO only for SCAs with a determined pricing framework (fixed or floor/ceiling). Based on committed volumes and minimum pricing. Inherently conservative. Revenue expected to well exceed RPO.
- FQ4 DRAM revenue a record $39.8 billion, 73% of total, +27% sequentially. Bit shipments up mid-single digit percentage range. Prices increased high teens percentage range.
- FQ4 NAND revenue a record $14.1 billion, 26% of total, +42% sequentially. Bit shipments increased approximately 10%. Prices increased approximately 30%.
- FQ4 capex $10.8 billion (release net figure is $10.77 billion). Customer cash deposits received in FQ4 $12.3 billion. Deposits on balance sheet at quarter end $12.7 billion.
- FQ1 guide matches the release: revenue $61.5B ± $1.5B, GM approximately 86.25%, opex approximately $2.06B, EPS $38.15 ± $1.
- Expect FQ1 to be the floor for gross margins in FY27. Higher gross margins beyond FQ1, with a more moderate rate of price increases.
- FQ1 capex around $11.5 billion. First-half FY27 capex approximately $25 billion. Second half higher. Construction capex growth rate meaningfully higher than equipment.

Not in this transcript: a point estimate for DRAM ASP (no "+17%" or "+18%"). "High teens" is the company's band. "탈사이클" does not appear.
