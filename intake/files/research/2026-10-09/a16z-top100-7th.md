## Methodology
- Web top 50 by monthly visits (Similarweb); mobile top 50 by MAU (Sensor Tower). GenAI must be central; incumbents eligible. NEW: NSFW-primary products excluded (would be >20% of web list).
- NEW spend ranking: YipitData, observed spend on U.S. consumer cards / U.S. e-receipt panel. "U.S.-only, drawn from panels rather than a census; should not be read as total company revenue." Gemini excluded from spend list (can't separate from Google consumer revenue). Data month mostly Aug 2026; tier data = YipitData Sep 10, 2026 workbook. Claude usage decline uses YipitData GLOBAL desktop panel.
- Agent numbers = founder/company claims or "reportedly" (Instinct: Noah Shinn), AssistantBenchmark (David Pawlan, n=1,502 group-chat messages).
