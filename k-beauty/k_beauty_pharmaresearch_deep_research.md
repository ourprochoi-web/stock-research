## Executive Summary

PharmaResearch is a Korean bio-materials company built on a single proprietary technology: DNA Optimizing Technology (DOT), which extracts and purifies polynucleotide (PN) from salmon milt. This underpins both the Rejuran medical device franchise and the Rejuran Cosmetic skincare brand. The company posted record 2025 revenues of 5,357억원 (+53% YoY) and 2,142억원 operating profit (+70% YoY), with OPM expanding from 36% to 40%. Q1 2026 hit another all-time quarterly high of 1,461억원 revenue (+25% YoY) and 573억원 operating profit (+28% YoY, OPM 39.2%). The central investment tension is whether the growth engine can continue to compound at 20-25% per year when: (a) the existing plant complex is near capacity, and (b) the transformative Plant 5 in Gangneung will not contribute meaningfully until 2027-2028.

### Plant 5 — The Capacity Unlock Event
- **Groundbreaking**: May 22, 2026
- **Phase 1** (pharmaceutical & medical device raw materials): Target completion 2027
- **Phase 2** (cosmetics production): Target completion 2028
- **Strategic rationale**: Company cited "proactive response to global market expansion and increasing product demand" — acknowledging supply pressure
- **Capacity multiples post-completion**: This is the most material figure. An 8x pharmaceutical and 5x medical device expansion implies the current four-plant complex is severely supply-constrained relative to long-term demand scenarios.

### Evidence of Demand Outpacing Supply
- The scale of the Plant 5 expansion (5x to 10x capacity multipliers) is strong indirect evidence that existing capacity is near saturation.
- The CG USA acquisition (Los Angeles ODM/OEM facility, 2026) was explicitly to address cosmetics "lead time and safety stock reduction" — direct supply-chain pressure signal.

- Amazon cosmetics revenue grew 700%+ YoY in 2025, pushing U.S. share of cosmetics exports from 33% (Q4 2025) to 48% (Q1 2026) — demand acceleration that outpaced domestic production planning.

[LIMITATION] No public disclosure of actual utilization rates for Plants 1-4. The capacity constraint evidence is structural/inferential rather than from disclosed utilization data. It is possible the company is expanding preemptively rather than reactively.
